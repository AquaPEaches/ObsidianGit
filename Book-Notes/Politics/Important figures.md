# Important figures



## Michael Romanov

[Russian History](./Russian%20History.md).

Was Tsar of Russia from 1613-1645, **founder** of the Romanov dynasty after the events of <u>Time of Troubles</u> following the death of Fyodor I.



Crowned at <mark>16 years </mark>old, gave his mother's relatives control in government affairs. They of course, took advantage of this opportunity for personal gain, but also, restored order in Russia, and made peace with both Poland and Sweden.
