# Russian History

## The Tsardom and Russian Empire(1480-1917):

<img src="https://cdn.britannica.com/95/80495-004-4C4501CA/Flag-Russian-Empire.jpg?w=400&h=300&c=crop" title="" alt="Russian Empire" width="223">

Recieved it's stardom when Russian nobility sought a new bloodline for monarchy. They elected <u>[Michael Romanov](./Important%20figures.md)</u>(1613), he then passed the reign to his son, Alexis at 1645 and then at 1676, Fyodor III took power.



All three were <mark>considered model Russian monarchs</mark> by the <u>Slavophiles</u> of the 19th century.



Notable events during the 17th century:

- The urban uprising
  
  - Concluded with the exile of **<u>Morozov</u>**

- The great peasant rebellion



**In Theory:** Russian monarchy had unlimited economic and legal power. The Tsar could make laws/change them, impose taxes, control the government, command militairy, ect. In actuality, the Tsar had much less ability to exercise that power across the entire Russian Empire.
